# HR-Workforce-Employee-Attrition-Analytics
Interactive HR analytics dashboard built using Google Looker Studio and IBM HR Analytics dataset.


## Project Overview
An interactive HR analytics dashboard built using Google Looker Studio and the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition dataset. The project analyzes workforce demographics, employee attrition patterns, job roles, departments, and salary trends to generate meaningful HR insights.

## Live Dashboard

Explore the interactive HR Workforce & Employee Attrition Dashboard:

[View Live Dashboard](https://datastudio.google.com/reporting/ab917fb2-869b-406c-81b9-a01b6e396fd5)

## Business Objectives
- Analyze overall employee attrition.
- Understand workforce demographics.
- Identify departments and job roles with higher attrition.
- Explore salary and employee experience trends.
- Support data-driven HR decision-making.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Google Looker Studio:** Interactive dashboard and data visualization.
- **Google Sheets:** Dataset connection and data handling.
- **IBM HR Analytics Dataset:** Workforce and employee attrition data.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Employees | 1,470 |
| Average Age | 36.92 |
| Average Monthly Income | 6,502.93 |
| Average Years at Company | 7.01 |
| Employee Attrition Rate | 16.12% |

## Dashboard Features
- HR Workforce Overview
- Attrition by Department
- Attrition by Job Role
- Attrition by Gender
- Attrition by Age Group
- Employee Attrition Status

## Dataset
IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition dataset containing 1,470 employee records and workforce-related attributes.

## Key Insights
- The overall employee attrition rate is approximately 16.12%.
- Research & Development has the largest workforce among the departments.
- The dashboard highlights differences in employee distribution across job roles.
- Gender and age-group visualizations provide additional workforce insights.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates how HR data can be transformed into an interactive analytics dashboard to monitor workforce trends and support informed business decisions.
